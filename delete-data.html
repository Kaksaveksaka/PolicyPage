Data Privacy

Data Deletion Request

This page provides instructions on how to delete data for games provided by Silver Hook, including Lava Stack (com.silverhook.stackAbrainrot) and Knife Slice 3D.

1. Deleting Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Our games do not require account registration or server-side logins. Your gameplay progression, scores, unlocked items, and settings are saved locally on your device.

How to delete local data:
  1. Open your Android device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps > See all apps.
  3. Select Lava Stack or Knife Slice 3D.
  4. Tap Storage & cache > Clear Storage (or uninstall the game).

2. Requesting Deletion of Telemetry / Analytics Data

Pseudonymous diagnostic and ad measurement telemetry processed by third-party SDKs (such as GameAnalytics or Google Mobile Ads) is keyed to temporary hardware identifiers.

To submit a data deletion inquiry or request deletion of data associated with your device identifier, send an email to:

Email: Kaksaveksaka@gmail.com
Subject line: Data Deletion Request - [Game Name]

Please specify the game title (e.g., Lava Stack or Knife Slice 3D) and any relevant anonymous identifier if available. Note: Never send sensitive financial or payment card information.
