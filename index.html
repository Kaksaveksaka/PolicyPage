Master Privacy Statement

Privacy Policy for Lava Stack & Silver Hook Games

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Silver Hook ("Silver Hook", "we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes casual games on mobile and web platforms, including Lava Stack (Application ID: com.silverhook.stackAbrainrot) and Knife Slice 3D. This Privacy Policy details our data practices across our game catalog.

Privacy Commitment: We respect your privacy. Our games do not require account registration or collect direct personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, or phone number). Game progress is stored locally on your device. Third-party SDKs process technical telemetry and advertising identifiers to provide game functionality, ad delivery, and diagnostic analytics.

1. Information We Handle

A. Local Device Storage

We store game progression data locally on your device (e.g. high scores, unlocked skins, audio settings). This data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game.

B. Third-Party Service Providers

Our integrated third-party partners process pseudonymous technical information:

  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: Processes Advertising IDs (AAID), device info, and ad interaction metrics to deliver, personalize, and measure mobile ads.
  • GameAnalytics: Processes pseudonymous device identifiers, crash logs, and gameplay events (e.g. run start, fail, score) to balance game difficulty and resolve bugs.
  • Playgama / Web Platforms: Processes platform session data when playing on supported web game portals.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles payment transactions for digital goods. We never store or receive payment card data.

2. Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GameAnalytics Privacy Notice
  • Playgama Privacy Policy

3. Data Deletion and User Controls

You can clear locally stored data by clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the game. To request deletion of telemetry records associated with your advertising identifier, visit our Data Deletion Instructions or email Kaksaveksaka@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

Our games are built for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
