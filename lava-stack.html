Privacy Policy for Lava Stack

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Lava Stack (Application ID: com.silverhook.stackAbrainrot) is a casual mobile and web game developed and operated by Silver Hook ("Silver Hook", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you download, install, or play Lava Stack on Android (Google Play) and supported web platforms.

Key Summary: We do not require account registration, and we do not collect or store personal identifiers like your name, email, or physical location on our own servers. Game progress and scores are saved locally on your device. Third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads and GameAnalytics) process technical and pseudonymous analytics and ad delivery data to support and maintain the game.

1. Information We Collect and Handle

A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Lava Stack does not require you to create an account or provide personal login credentials. The game stores gameplay preferences and states locally on your device via local storage / app preferences:

  • High scores, run progression, and best records.
  • In-game currency and cosmetic/character unlock statuses.
  • User settings (audio, music, SFX volume, and graphics toggles).

B. Information Processed by Third-Party Service Providers

When you play Lava Stack, certain third-party libraries and services integrated into the app may automatically process pseudonymous device and usage data:

  • Analytics Data (GameAnalytics): To detect bugs, monitor game balance, and improve gameplay stability, GameAnalytics may process pseudonymous device identifiers, device model, operating system version, session start/end timestamps, crash diagnostics, and in-game progression events (such as run start, run end, and scores).
  • Advertising Data (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob): The Android version of Lava Stack may display ads served by Google Mobile Ads. Google may process your device's Advertising Identifier (such as Google Play Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, general device hardware details, app interaction information, and ad performance data for ad delivery, frequency capping, attribution, and fraud detection.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): If in-app purchases are offered (such as Ad-Free upgrades), transaction processing is managed directly by Google Play. Silver Hook never receives, processes, or stores your credit card or financial details. We only receive a confirmation token verifying your purchased entitlement to unlock features.
  • Web Platform Data (Playgama / Partner Portals): When Lava Stack is played via web browsers on platform partners (such as Playgama, CrazyGames, or MSN), the platform bridge and hosting services may process session data, browser identifiers, and platform-specific saves in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Information handled through the game is strictly used for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To deliver, execute, and maintain core gameplay mechanics and save game state.
  • To provide and measure relevant advertisements, preventing fraud and abusive traffic.
  • To monitor game stability, analyze crash reports, and troubleshoot technical errors.
  • To understand player progression and improve game features and difficulty balancing.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and app store developer policies.

3. Advertising and In-House Content

Lava Stack displays ads served by Google Mobile Ads on mobile platforms. You can control or reset your advertising identifiers at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

We may also display in-house promotional notices for our other titles. These in-house creatives are bundled locally with the game assets and do not track or transmit personal data.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our integrated third-party service providers operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play & Google Mobile Ads)
  • GameAnalytics Privacy Notice (Game analytics and crash monitoring)
  • Playgama Privacy Policy (Web platform integration)

5. Data Retention, User Rights, and Deletion

Local Data: All local game records, unlocks, and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app cache/data in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Third-Party Data: Third-party SDK partners retain technical and telemetry data according to their individual retention policies and legal retention mandates.

Deletion Requests: If you wish to submit a data deletion inquiry or have privacy questions regarding data associated with your device, please visit our Data Deletion Instructions Page or contact us directly by email.

6. Children's Privacy

Lava Stack is designed as a casual game for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal data has been inadvertently collected from your child, please contact us immediately so we can take prompt corrective action.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard data processed in connection with Lava Stack. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage system is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates to our game features, integrations, or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective date".
